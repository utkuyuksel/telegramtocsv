1. What the Service does

TelegramtoCSV.com is a utility that exports the publicly visible message history of public Telegram channels into a downloadable CSV (comma-separated values) or Excel (.xlsx) file. The Service connects to Telegram using its public API and downloads only data that any Telegram user could already read by visiting the channel.

The Service is provided as-is, on a best-effort basis. We do not guarantee that every channel can be exported, that every export will be complete, or that the Service will always be available.
